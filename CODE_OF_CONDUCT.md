# Code of Conduct

Escalate exists to help people who were treated unfairly. We expect the same fairness from
everyone in this project: maintainers, contributors and users.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are dealing with a stressful money problem, and many
  are writing in their second or third language.
- Assume good faith, and give and take feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect people of every background, country, language, religion, gender, sexuality,
  disability, age and level of experience.
- Share legal information carefully: cite official sources, and never present a guess as fact.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or discriminatory language, in public or in private.
- Posting someone's personal information (names, addresses, case details) without consent.
- Using Escalate, or asking for help, to harass a person or business, file false complaints or
  run mass campaigns against one recipient.
- Spam, or promoting paid "recovery" services to scam victims.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through a private GitHub security advisory or by contacting a
maintainer directly. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content,
and warn, suspend or ban anyone who breaks this code, in this repository and in project spaces.
